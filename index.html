What is FundAWish?

Someone out there just needs a coffee. Or a bus fare. Or twenty minutes of relief from a week that's been too heavy.

FundAWish is where you post that small wish. No campaign. No pitch deck. Just a real moment and a real ask.

And someone who woke up feeling generous that morning sends it directly. No platform taking a cut. No middleman. Just two people.
